1. Why is the project named vanling?

   It stems from an admiration for [chobits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chobits) (original inspiration) and [Planetarian: The Reverie of a Little Planet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planetarian:_The_Reverie_of_a_Little_Planet).
